Connect Gemini AI

TazAI works right away in built-in mode. To unlock the real Gemini AI — deeper answers, photo reading and full document analysis — add your own free Gemini API key. It takes about a minute:

  1. Step 1: Open Google AI Studio and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Step 2: Click “Create API Key” / “Get API Key”.
  3. Step 3: Copy the key — it starts with AIza… or AQ.…
  4. Step 4: Paste it below and tap Save.
Get a free key — Google AI Studio
Privacy: Your key is stored only in this browser, on this device. It is never sent to anyone else — it is only used to fetch answers for you from Google's Gemini servers. Without a key, TazAI still works in built-in mode.

Install TazAI

Add TazAI to your home screen — it opens like an app.

Great — your browser can install TazAI as an app. It takes a few seconds, and the TazAI icon will appear on your home screen.

  1. Android (Chrome): Open the ⋮ menu at the top-right of the browser → tap “Install app” or “Add to Home screen” → confirm Install.
  2. iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share button → “Add to Home Screen” → tap Add.
  3. Once installed, a TazAI icon appears on your home screen — open it from there, just like an app.
